FAQ

  • Do I need an account? No. All data stays on your iPhone.
  • How does blocking work? Earnlock uses an iOS Shortcuts personal automation ("When App is Opened" → Earnlock "Check Access"). Open Earnlock → Settings → Shortcuts setup for step-by-step instructions.
  • How do I cancel Earnlock Pro? iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Earnlock.
  • How do I erase my data? Earnlock → Settings → Erase all data.
  • Privacy? See our Privacy Policy.
