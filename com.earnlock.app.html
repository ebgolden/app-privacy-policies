Earnlock Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 24, 2026 · Last updated: October 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Earnlock ("we", "us", "the App") collects, how it's used, and your rights. Earnlock is published by Edward Goldenberg. Contact: edwardgoldenberg@gmail.com.

Information We Collect

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information from users. Earnlock has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it does not communicate with any server operated by us. The App operates entirely on your device.

Data stored only on your iPhone

Your tasks, Time Bank history, streaks, settings, the list of apps you choose to block, and the record of when those apps were opened are stored locally on your device only. Deleting the App, or using Settings → Erase all data, removes them.

Camera

If you choose photo proof for a task, the App uses the camera only while you take the photo. Proof photos are saved inside the App on your iPhone and are never uploaded or shared. The App does not access your photo library and does not use the microphone.

Motion & Fitness

Step-goal tasks read your step count from your iPhone's motion sensor, starting when you tap Start. Step data is used only to check whether the goal was met and never leaves your device.

Partner PIN

The optional accountability-partner PIN is stored as a salted hash in the iOS Keychain on your device. The PIN itself is never stored or transmitted.

Notifications

Reminders and session alerts are scheduled locally on your iPhone. The App does not use a push-notification server.

Purchases

Earnlock Pro subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not see or store your payment details. The App only learns from StoreKit whether your subscription is active.

How We Use Information

  • To provide and maintain the App's functionality, entirely on your device.

Data Sharing

We do not share any data because we do not collect any. We do not sell or rent personal information to anyone.

Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction (GDPR in Europe, CCPA in California, similar laws elsewhere) you may have the right to access or delete data held about you. Because all Earnlock data stays on your device, you can delete it at any time with Settings → Erase all data or by deleting the App. For any questions, email edwardgoldenberg@gmail.com.

Children's Privacy

Earnlock is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

Data Retention

We do not retain any data because we do not collect any. Data on your device is kept until you erase it or delete the App.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email edwardgoldenberg@gmail.com.
